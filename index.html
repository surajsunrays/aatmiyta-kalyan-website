14 APRIL · A LEGACY THAT LIVES ON

Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Jayanti

Educate. Organize. Agitate.

On 14 April, we honour Babasaheb with gratitude and renew our commitment to education, equality, and human dignity. His message invites us to carry these values into everyday life, beyond a single day of celebration.

Put these values into practice ↓
01 / KNOWLEDGE

Educate

Learn, question, and share knowledge. Education helps us think independently, understand our rights, and open doors for others.

Start with a shared reading, help a student learn, or discuss the values of the Constitution.

02 / SOLIDARITY

Organize

Bring people together with a shared purpose. Lasting change grows through cooperation, mutual respect, and a community where everyone can contribute.

Form a study circle, volunteer together, or support a shared community initiative.

03 / COURAGE

Agitate

Raise your voice against discrimination and injustice. Through informed dialogue and peaceful, democratic action, stand up for equal dignity and opportunity.

Ask thoughtful questions, challenge prejudice respectfully, and listen to those whose voices go unheard.

Remember together. Move forward together.

Connect with our team at the Buddha Vihar to learn about the local Jayanti programme and ways to help. Programme dates and timings are confirmed locally.
